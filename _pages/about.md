Work
======  
  * Data Scientist  
    [OCBC AI Lab](https://www.ocbc.com), Singapore, Jun 2022 - present  
    * Productionized VLM-based document extraction pipelines (invoice, term sheet, classification) on OCBC LLM Cluster with an end-user UI, rolled out company-wide; ~92–95% accuracy across 3 business units.  
    * Sole contributor in designing and deploying a vLLM/SGLang + LiteLLM inference stack on GPU cluster, enabling day-1 LLM/VLM model support for internal business units company-wide.  
    * Led OCR-based key field extraction from 2M+ historical KYC scanned documents using Qwen3.6 and PaddleOCR, contributing structured data to support a Client Lifetime Management system.  
    * Built NLP model services for Group Legal & Compliance (NER-based term labelling, 5+ FTEs saved) and Treasury Operations (MiniLM SWIFT message classification integrated with PEGA, 3 FTEs saved).  
    * Built a Python package for Singapore and Malaysia reconciliation process, onboarding 300+ term sheet templates across 5 product types, processing 1k+ trades monthly.
