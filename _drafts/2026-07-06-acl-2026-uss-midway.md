The ACL 2026 social event took place on **Monday, July 6** aboard the **USS Midway Museum**, a decommissioned aircraft carrier turned floating museum on San Diego Bay, a short walk from the conference hotel.

The evening ran from 6:30 to 10:00 PM. Guests could walk the flight deck among restored aircraft, try flight simulators, look around the crew quarters and engine rooms, and talk to the museum docents, with music, food and drinks, and the bay at sunset.
