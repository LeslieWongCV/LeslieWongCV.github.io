This blog went quiet after August 2022. Here is a short catch-up on what happened since, and what to expect next.

## What changed

- **2022:** Finished my M.Sc. in Computer Control & Automation at Nanyang Technological University and joined OCBC in Singapore.
- **Now:** Data Scientist at OCBC AI Lab, working on Document AI: OCR, vision-language models and information extraction for financial documents.

Most of my day-to-day work is internal, so I can't write about it here. What I *can* share are the things that went through peer review or open competitions.

## Public work since 2022

- **ACL 2026 (Industry Track):** *A Multistage Extraction Pipeline for Long Scanned Financial Documents.* See the [ACL post](/posts/2026/7/acl-2026-industry-track/).
- **DocInsights @ EMNLP 2026:** two papers, one on calibrated confidence for straight-through processing and one shared-task paper for Dr.DocBench. See the [DocInsights post](/posts/2026/10/docinsights-emnlp-2026/).
- **Dr.DocBench Challenge:** 3rd place overall. See the [write-up](/posts/2026/10/drdocbench-third-place/).
- **arXiv:** [*Multi-Stage Field Extraction of Financial Documents with OCR and Compact Vision-Language Models*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.23066).

## And some travel

Two conferences came with two trips: a Highway 1 road trip after ACL in California, and Vienna and Budapest after EMNLP.
Photos and notes are in the [Highway 1 post](/posts/2026/7/california-highway-1/) and the [Vienna & Budapest post](/posts/2026/11/vienna-budapest/).

## What's next

I plan to write more about the public side of Document AI: paper notes, benchmark write-ups and small experiments, in the style of my earlier posts here.
